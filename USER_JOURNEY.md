# USER JOURNEY

1. The player opens the game and immediately understands the escape objective.
2. The player enters a ruined district and starts exploring with a follow camera.
3. The player manages health and stamina while reading danger zones and moving drones.
4. The player discovers the safe zone only after getting close enough.
5. The player escapes one district and enters a harder one.
6. The player either fails quickly and retries, or survives all levels and reaches final safety.
